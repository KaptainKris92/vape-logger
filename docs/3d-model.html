Assembly order

  1. Solder the 100 kΩ resistor from D1 to GND on the XIAO.
  2. Solder a red wire from 3V3 and a marked black wire from D1, then solder those to the two FSR pins.
  3. Upload the press test and check presses show up before anything is stuck down.
  4. Solder the JST socket half to BAT+ and BAT− with the battery unplugged, then check polarity.
  5. Put the battery and JST socket in the far end of the printed tray, and the XIAO on 1 mm foam tape at the USB end with its port in the notch.
  6. Run the FSR wires out through the slot that faces the fire button, and press the lid on.
  7. Tape the case to the back panel with double-sided foam tape, USB-C pointing up. Lay the FSR disc on the fire button and tape the tail round the edge.

Approximate in this model: the vape's exact shape and the position of its fire button (check against yours), the length of the FSR tail, and where the BAT pads sit on the XIAO's underside. Use Seeed's pinout picture for the real pad positions.
