Wiring diagram

Three connections for the sensor and two for the battery. Pin names match the labels printed on the XIAO.

Check the JST polarity with the multimeter before the battery goes on: the battery's red wire must end up at BAT+.

Assembly order

  1. Solder the 100 kΩ resistor from D1 to GND on the XIAO.
  2. Solder a red wire from 3V3 and a green wire from D1, then solder those to the two FSR pins.
  3. Upload the press test and check presses show up before anything is stuck down.
  4. Solder the JST socket half to BAT+ and BAT− with the battery unplugged, then check polarity.
  5. Stick the battery to the back panel with foam tape, then the XIAO on top with a layer of tape between them.
  6. Lay the FSR disc on the fire button and tape the tail round the edge.

Approximate in this model: the vape's exact shape and the position of its fire button (check against yours), the length of the FSR tail, and where the BAT pads sit on the XIAO's underside. Use Seeed's pinout picture for the real pad positions.
